Before the first launch

The builds aren't signed with a paid certificate, so your system may ask for confirmation.

Windows

If SmartScreen shows up, click “More info” → “Run anyway”.

macOS

Open the app with right click → “Open”, or allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Linux

Make the file executable: chmod +x OffPrism-Linux-x86_64.AppImage
