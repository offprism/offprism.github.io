About

What is OffPrism

OffPrism is a fork of Prism Launcher, which itself builds on PolyMC and MultiMC. We added offline play without a Microsoft account, Ely.by and authlib-injector accounts with skins in game, the Glass theme and our own auto-updates. Everything else — instances, mods, modpacks — works exactly like in Prism.

We are not affiliated with the Prism Launcher team. Please don't send them bug reports about OffPrism — use our issue tracker instead.

Privacy

  • We don't collect your data and don't show ads.
  • Ely.by and authlib-injector passwords are never stored — the launcher keeps only the session token.
